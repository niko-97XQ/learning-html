Vectorized Tree-Graph Hybrid Retrieval for Embodied Spatial Memory Retrieval

Qiang Xu · Jingyi Dai · Xinwei Zhang

For node retrieval in hierarchical tree-graph memory, we filter the object or region level according to the query type, then rerank results with parent–child context to reduce cross-level interference and improve the retrieval quality of relevant scene information.

NYC Citi Bike Flow Prediction

Can POI information provide a useful signal for spatio-temporal prediction?

Responsible for cleaning about 4.98 million trip records, spatio-temporal aggregation and weather alignment to build grid-based flow data; built a POI feature module from OpenStreetMap and adapted a GCN model.

Comparing no-POI, real-POI and shuffled-POI settings to analyze errors and iterate on the prediction method. Experiments are still running; results will be written up after validation.

  • POI

2026.10 — Present

Generative AI Collaboration in Undergraduate Math Modeling

How do students adopt AI, and how do they verify it?

Based on a survey of 154 participants in 61 teams from a campus mathematical modeling contest, studying idea formation, AI content adoption and result verification.

Comparing adoption patterns in modeling and programming tasks, and mapping how AI review combines with manual verification such as reading, calculation and testing. Survey analysis is in progress.

  • Survey analysis
  • Math modeling
